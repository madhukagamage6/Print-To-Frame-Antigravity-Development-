# Mathematical Composition & Grid Systems

Visual harmony in masterwork canvas design is rooted in rigorous mathematical proportions. This guide details geometric systems, harmonic step-ratios, and spatial division techniques.

---

## 1. Classical Proportions & Dynamic Symmetry

### 1. The Golden Ratio ($\Phi \approx 1.618$)
- **Golden Section Subdivisions**: Divide canvas along $W / 1.618$ and $H / 1.618$ to place focal geometric anchors.
- **Harmonic Spirals**: Spiral growth curves computed via polar coordinates:
  $$r = a \cdot e^{b\theta}$$
  Where $b = \frac{\ln(\Phi)}{\pi/2} \approx 0.30635$.

### 2. Root Rectangles (Dynamic Symmetry)
- **$\sqrt{2}$ Rectangle (1 : 1.414)**: ISO paper standard (A4, A3, A2). Any sub-rectangle created by halving possesses identical aspect ratio.
- **$\sqrt{3}$ Rectangle (1 : 1.732)**: Tripartite equilibrium, ideal for triptych compositions and 3-axis isometric projections.
- **$\sqrt{5}$ Rectangle (1 : 2.236)**: Elongated panoramic ratios with natural dual golden-section intersections.

## 2. Grid Architectures

### 1. Swiss 12-Column Modular Grid
- **Total Columns**: 12 equal-width columns.
- **Outer Margin**: 48px to 80px.
- **Column Gaps (Gutters)**: 16px to 24px.
- **Horizontal Hanglines / Baselines**: 8px baseline grid with major horizontal datum marks every 96px.

### 2. The Asymmetric Quadrant System
Divide canvas into 4 unequal quadrants using an offset focal axis ($62\% / 38\%$ split):
- **Primary Field (Top-Left 62% x 62%)**: Monumental graphic or generative line form.
- **Secondary Field (Bottom-Left 62% x 38%)**: Dark dense mass or high-frequency texture.
- **Annotation Column (Right 38% x 100%)**: Monospaced coordinate tags, technical dimension lines, and minimal typographic titles.

---

## 3. Systematic Annotation & Technical Markings

To give abstract canvas art the precision of an authoritative scientific or architectural drafting document:

1. **Registration & Target Marks**:
   - `⌖` Axis crosshairs at major grid intersections.
   - `⊕` Center datum markers at canvas center and quadrant centers.
2. **Dimension Lines & Ticks**:
   - 0.5px hairline lines with outward $45^\circ$ end ticks measuring spatial blocks.
3. **Coordinate & Classification Badges**:
   - Set in monospaced typography (`JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code`) at 10px-12px.
   - Example formatting: `LOC: 34.0522°N / 118.2437°W`, `SCALE: 1:5000`, `TOLERANCE: ±0.002`, `FIG. 04-A`.
4. **Color Swatch Key**:
   - Row of 4-6 small geometric swatches (6px squares or circles) in bottom corner displaying the composition's chromatic palette.
