# Canvas Design Skill

The `canvas-design` skill guides the creation of masterwork-level static visual art, posters, architectural blueprints, generative graphics, and visual design philosophies. It transforms abstract ideas into museum-grade artifacts composed of **90% visual composition and 10% essential, highly disciplined typography**.

---

## Two-Phase Creation Architecture

Every canvas design project executes in two distinct phases:

```
[Phase 1: Design Philosophy Manifesto (.md)]
                    │
                    ▼
[Phase 2: Visual Canvas Masterpiece (.svg / .png / .pdf)]
```

---

## Phase 1: Design Philosophy Creation (`.md` Manifesto)

Before rendering visual marks, articulate a 4-to-6 paragraph design philosophy / aesthetic movement manifesto:

1. **Name the Movement**: 1–2 evocative words (e.g., *"Concrete Constructivism"*, *"Chromatic Silence"*, *"Kinetic Blueprint"*, *"Tectonic Pulse"*).
2. **Articulate the Manifesto (4–6 paragraphs)**:
   - **Form & Spatial Tension**: How mass, void, and geometry interact across the 2D plane.
   - **Chromatic Calibration**: The exact psychological and optical relationship of the palette (tonal shifts, contrast ratios, pigment temperature).
   - **Typographic Geometry**: How letters and words act as structural architectural elements rather than reading blocks.
   - **Craftsmanship & Precision**: Rigorous standards of execution, mathematical balance, and finished perfection.
3. **Avoid Redundancy**: Mention each concept once with depth. Avoid generic filler.

---

## Phase 2: Canvas Creation (`.svg` / `.png` / `.pdf`)

Using the manifesto, render the visual artifact on the canvas:

### 1. Spatial Structure & Grid Composition
- Use mathematical proportions: Golden Ratio ($\Phi = 1.618$), $\sqrt{2}$ ISO grid, or Swiss 12-column modular grid.
- Establish dramatic scale variance: monumental macro shapes paired with intricate micro details.
- Provide generous negative space (at least 20%–40% breathing room).

### 2. Systematic Technical Language & Aesthetics
- Incorporate subtle systematic references: coordinate grids, datum lines, dimension ticks, registration crosshairs (`⌖`, `⊕`), and color calibration swatches.
- Treat the canvas with the reverence of a master scientific drafting plate or architectural specification sheet.

### 3. Disciplined Minimalist Typography
- Typography is sparse, contextual, and pristine: short titles, datum coordinates, index numbers, or catalog markers.
- **Strict Constraint**: Never fill the canvas with explanatory paragraphs. Text is a visual accent.
- Ensure strict margin safety: no text or graphic element touches or clips canvas borders.

## Core Aesthetics & Movements Quick Catalog

| Movement | Dominant Palette | Geometry & Mood | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concrete Constructivism** | Charcoal, Vermilion, Cobalt, Ochre | Monumental color blocks, angular cuts, heavy mass | Architectural posters, exhibition art |
| **Chromatic Interaction** | Warm Amber, Sky Cyan, Slate Gray | Concentric nested fields, optical hue resonance | Fine art prints, music albums |
| **Kinetic Blueprint** | Blueprint Cyan, Steel Slate, Obsidian | CAD wireframes, dimension ticks, datum marks | ERP engineering art, BIM diagrams |
| **Generative Minimalist** | Ink Black, Ivory, Fine Gold hairlines | Algorithmic waves, moiré arcs, dense harmonic lines | Mathematical posters, bookplates |
| **Swiss Rationalism** | Stark White, Pure Black, Safety Red | Asymmetrical modular grid, precise Grotesk type | International design exhibitions |
| **Wabi-Sabi Silence** | Earthen Clay, Celadon, Washed Sumi | Vast open void (Ma), single organic silhouette | Contemplative and luxury art |

## Quality Checklist Before Output

- [ ] Is the design philosophy saved as a distinct `.md` file?
- [ ] Is the visual canvas exported in high-DPI `.svg`, `.png`, or `.pdf`?
- [ ] Does the visual design dominate 90% of the canvas with text at ≤ 10%?
- [ ] Are all margins safe with zero overlapping or clipped elements?
- [ ] Is the chromatic palette tightly controlled (3–5 intentional colors)?
- [ ] Does the piece feel like a labor of dozens of hours by a master craftsman?
